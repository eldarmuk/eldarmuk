# Hi, I’m Eldar Mukhtarov

**Undergraduate Research Lead at Lodz University of Technology · AI for health and applied AI systems**

I study how AI can help make sense of health data, with current work on acute health events in older adults using wearable sensors. I also build AI applications. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Łódź and am continuing engineering studies at Lodz University of Technology. My next academic goal is a PhD in AI for medical and health applications.

## Research direction

My work connects signal processing and machine learning with health research. Earlier projects in acoustic analysis and sound classification led into my current work on longitudinal wearable data. Alongside this, I explore reinforcement learning for sequential decision-making.

- **[AI-CVD](https://github.com/eldarmuk/AI-CVD) — my main research project.** Data preparation, physiological and circadian features, anomaly detection, model evaluation and interpretability. First author of an [AIME 2026 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-30710-1_17), with Prof. Krzysztof Grudzień. An extended study on personalized detection is in preparation. The repository includes later experiments as well as work related to the paper; see its reproducibility notes. [Research overview](https://eldarmukhtar.ovh/projects/wearable-health).
- **[Reinforcement learning in MicroRTS](https://eldarmukhtar.ovh/projects/rl) — ongoing research.** Sequential multi-target decision-making with Dr. Piotr Duch. The repository is private; the portfolio provides a public overview.
- **[Acoustic signal processing and classification](https://eldarmukhtar.ovh/research) — earlier research.** My 2025 B.Sc. thesis and work on time–frequency methods form part of my technical foundation.
